    App install

    1. Remove the old app: long-press its icon › App info › Uninstall (or Android Settings › Apps › Tasks › Uninstall).
    2. In Chrome, open this site, tap below to reset its app files, then fully close Chrome (swipe it away in recent apps).
    3. Reopen this site in Chrome: tap ⋮ › Install app (or "Add to Home screen" › Install). If it still says installed, restart the phone and try once more.

    Clears this site's offline copies and background workers (Tasks, Journal, Research, Guild) and reloads. Your key, settings, tasks and journal stay.
