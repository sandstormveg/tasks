# Research

Your private research system, written by the Research app (the § tab in Tasks):

- **Deposit** — anything you drop in (a link, a quote, a thought) lands here first, with suggestions for
  which board or piece it belongs with.
- **Library** — things you mean to read, are reading and have read: tags, why you saved each one, what
  you expected, what surprised you, notes and exact quotes.
- **Writing** — your pieces (a grant proposal, an essay), each with its own corkboard.
- **Corkboards** — notes, sources and Infodex cards pinned to a board and tied with string: **red** while a
  connection is noticed but not worked out, **synthesised** once you've written how the two connect.
  Frames are regions with an address (domain, perspective, scale, time); a curator checks connections
  against the Atlas's rules.

| Path | What | Who writes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sources/YYYY/MM/` | One file per thing you saved (including deposited thoughts) | You, in the app (Claude can add sources and change status/⭐/tags) |
| `boards/<board>.md`, `boards/<board>/pins/`, `boards/<board>/strings/` | A board, its pins, its strings | You; Claude can add pins and red strings, marked as its own |
| `cards/card-Q….md` | An Infodex card: its Wikidata snapshot, and your entries about it | You (Claude can make the card, never write your entries) |
| `writing/` | One file per piece; the body is your draft | You (Claude can start a piece, never write the draft) |
| `tools/research.mjs` | Query tool for Claude sessions | Generated by the app — don't edit |
| `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/skills/research/` | How Claude should work with this folder | Generated by the app |

A source file looks like this:

```markdown
---
id: s-20261101-0930-k7q2
type: source
kind: paper
title: "Risks of astronomical future suffering"
url: https://…
status: read          # inbox (the Deposit) | to-read | reading | read | dropped | filed
favourite: true
tags: [s-risk, ethics]
added: 2026-11-01T09:30:00+07:00
why: "Foundational for the reading group"
expect: "Argues s-risks outweigh extinction risks"
surprised: "How much rests on the tractability argument"
quotes:
  - text: "…"
    where: "§3"
visibility: private
---
My notes, in Markdown.
```

Links only for now: no PDFs or files are stored here.

A string file (`boards/<board>/strings/r-….md`) has `from`, `to`, `state: red | synthesised`, and when
synthesised your words in its body, plus optionally `tier`, `holds_in` and `breaks_at`.
